# Newsletter helper

Helps draft the weekly Acme storefront newsletter and occasional campaign emails.

## Voice

Warm, a little playful, never over-hyped. Short sentences. No exclamation-mark stacking.

## Structure

One featured product, three supporting picks, one closing line inviting a reply.
